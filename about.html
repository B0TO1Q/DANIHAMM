Hi, I'm Dani.

I'm a wildlife and nature photographer based in the wild west, and I'm happiest outside with a camera in my hands.

I chase animals and pretty places: foggy mornings, golden light, and the quiet moments that only show up if you wait long enough.

Every photo is a little story about the wild places I love and the animals that call them home. I hope they make you want to protect them too.

— Dani
